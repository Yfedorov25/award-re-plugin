Inspired Architecture

A standalone crystal building soaring above the treetops. A secluded tower resembling a glassy waterfall with flowing cascades of greenery-framed loggia.

Each floor reflects boundless perspectives in its glistening waves, inviting you to look farther with a fuller palette of possibilities.

Tabanlioglu, the renowned architectural bureau, emphasizes the bold asymmetry of balconies in a chessboard pattern. Three vertical partitions divide the translucent façade, creating the effect of weightless volume. Freedom of expression, elegance of intelligence, and visual lightness.
