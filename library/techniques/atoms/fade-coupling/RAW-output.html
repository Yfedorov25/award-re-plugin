Essence of contemplation

Willow

Willow sits in the quiet west of the city, held between parkland and the embankment. Mornings arrive through the trees; evenings leave along the water. Nothing here insists — everything simply stays.

The gardens are planted in layers, so that no season leaves the grounds bare. What blooms is allowed to lean; what fades is allowed to rest.

Grounds — north walk

A gravel line runs the full length of the estate, unlit by design. Residents learn it by footfall, the way one learns a house at night.

Interiors keep to stone, oak and unbleached linen. Their only ornament is the green that stands outside every window.

A closing note

The estate does not ask to be remembered.
It asks to be returned to.

End of study
