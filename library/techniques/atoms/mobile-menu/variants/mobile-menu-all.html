Verdure — Mobile Menu Full-screen overlay atom · 390 × 844 · three takes on the open animation + orchid
A

Slide down

Panel drops in from the top; list items stagger up behind a line-mask; the orchid fades up with a gentle scale-settle. Clean and quiet.

B

Bright-green bloom

Panel fades as the floor glow blooms up from the bottom; the orchid and every blossom unfurl from nothing; list rises with opacity. More organic.

C

Designer's cut

A light sweep crosses the panel on open, the marker underlines the tapped item, and the orchid drifts in a slow idle-sway that runs off the open clock.

Each frame is a self-contained atom (inline CSS + JS, GSAP + Lenis). Tap the burger inside a frame, or drive it from here — the controls call openMenu() / closeMenu() on each iframe. Tapping a section highlights it (no navigation). Same law across all three; only the open choreography and orchid treatment vary.
