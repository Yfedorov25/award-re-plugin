Atelier

Craft in quiet proportion

Our rooms are drawn around the way a day actually unfolds — morning light on pale stone, the weight of a linen curtain, a chair worn soft by use. Natural materials are chosen to age with the house: oak, travertine, brushed metal and textured plaster, composed for an unhurried, high‑comfort life.

The residences — interior programme

The Atelier interior programme was developed with the Harven studio to carry one calm register through every residence. Muted palettes, arched portals and smooth curves let each room flow into the next, while oak, honed stone and softened light keep the whole house quiet. Every residence is delivered turnkey — finished to the last hinge — so the first evening in a new home already feels like the hundredth.
