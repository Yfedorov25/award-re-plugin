Each plate opens bottom-up, its boundary travelling with your scroll.

Openings are measured, not announced. A wall gives way exactly as far as the light asks it to, and no further — perspective arrives by degrees.

What the eye keeps is the reveal itself — the slow hem of shadow lifting from stone, the settled stillness after.
