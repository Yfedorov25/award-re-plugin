Room One — East Wing01 / 06

Dawn Sand

First light through linen curtains. A low bed on warm terracotta tile, the shoreline a two‑minute walk past the dune grass. Mornings here begin slowly, with coffee and salt air.

Room Two — Sea Terrace02 / 06

Sea Slate

Deep blue‑green walls the exact colour of the bay before a storm. A private terrace hangs over the water; at high tide you can hear it moving beneath the floorboards.

Room Three — Garden Side03 / 06

Pine

Shaded by the old coastal pines, this room smells faintly of resin and rain. Green‑lacquered shutters, a writing desk, and nothing on the walls but afternoon shadow.

Room Four — West Balcony04 / 06

Coral Evening

Built to face the sunset. The plaster holds the day's heat until midnight, and the balcony rail is worn smooth by forty summers of elbows and aperitifs.

Room Five — Upper Floor05 / 06

Night Navy

The quietest room in the house. Skylight over the bed, no curtains needed — only the harbour beacons and, on clear nights, more stars than seems reasonable.

Room Six — Ground Floor06 / 06

Fog Cream

Pale walls, pale stone, a fireplace for the misted‑over mornings when the sea disappears entirely. The kind of room people extend their stay for.
