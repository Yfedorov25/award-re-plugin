Commonwealth Partnership forms a management company capable of delivering premium service across the entire office lifecycle — from tenant acquisition and design to day-to-day administration.

Brokerage

  • Tenant search and acquisition;
  • Property presentation;
  • Creation of marketing materials;
  • Promotion of the property across advertising platforms;
  • Commercial negotiations in the owner’s interests;
  • Legal support.

Lease management

  • Support of lease processes;
  • Administration of lease agreements, renewals, and indexation;
  • Document management;
  • Accounting and legal support of lease relations;
  • Management, accounting, and tax reporting.

Interior design

Development of design concepts, project documentation, and visualizations for the owner’s property.

Fit-out project management

Provision of supervision and control throughout fit-out project execution.

Interior fit-out and renovation

IT infrastructure support and maintenance

Reception service organization and management

Office equipment maintenance

24/7 helpdesk service and support hotline

Marketing support and services

Mail and courier services
