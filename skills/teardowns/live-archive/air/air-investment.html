Investing in AIR offices

Key advantages

Investing in office spaces at the AIR business center offers owners strong opportunities for stable income and capital growth.

A prime location, high demand for Class A office space, and well-designed infrastructure make the project particularly attractive to investors.

  • High demand

    In the most sought-after locations, such as Khodynskoye Field, the supply of Class A office space is limited — new inventory is quickly absorbed by tenants. According to NF Group, the overall vacancy rate for completed Class A office space in the Leningradsky corridor, which includes Khodynskoye Field, stands at 2.9%.

  • Strong tenant base

    Corporate clients are more likely to enter into long-term lease agreements and demonstrate high payment reliability. Lease contracts are typically signed for an average term of five years or more. The longer a single tenant occupies a space, the more stable the cash flow becomes and the lower the time and administrative costs associated with finding new tenants. Tenant reliability is assessed through an analysis of their financial performance.

  • Tenants prefer Shell & Core

    Tenants typically prefer spaces without fit-out, allowing them to design the interior in their own style.

  • Ease of management

    The scope of a professional business center management company is extensive. The management company provides in-house services and brokerage, oversees tenant relations, and can even be entrusted with fit-out works and furniture procurement.

  • Guaranteed investment security Turn on screen reader support

    Office sales at AIR are conducted under shared construction participation agreements. Each transaction is securely protected by law through mandatory state registration of contracts.
