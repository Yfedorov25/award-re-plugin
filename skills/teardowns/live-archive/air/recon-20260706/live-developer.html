ADM Bureau
Architecture

One of leading architectural studios, founded in 2006 by Andrey Romanov and Ekaterina Kuznetsova. The name ADM — “Architectural Dialogue with the Metropolis” — reflects its core focus. The bureau specializes in designing buildings for the modern city.

haast
Interiors

Founded in 2016, the bureau designs private, office, and public spaces, delivering interior and architectural solutions of any complexity — from large business centers to private homes. Process discipline, team talent, and up-to-date technical expertise are central to the work. Each project is an opportunity to create distinctive solutions and raise the bar for the profession.

Technical consultant

A provider of a full range of high-quality property management services, backed by years of hands-on experience and a team of over 1,100 professionals.
