Architecture
of efficiency

Aesthetics as the highest form of pragmatism.
The architecture of AIR embodies the rhythm of the present, reflecting the social and business dynamics of the capital.

The distinctive style of AIR was created by ADM, a leading architectural studio specializing in contemporary urban design.

1 / 3

Panoramic curved glazing supports natural daylight and visually expands the space. Energy-efficient windows with Schüco profiles and special glass coatings retain heat and protect interiors from excessive sunlight.

At a structural level, AIR supports flexible layouts and unconventional ideas. A column grid of 8.4 × 8.4 meters in a single span enables efficient planning solutions.

From the windows of the view offices, panoramic vistas of city towers and Khodynskoye Field Park unfold.

Everything revolves around you

In typical office buildings, floor plans are utilitarian and centered around a core that houses staircases, elevator shafts, and engineering systems. At AIR, this challenge is solved in an elegant, high-tech way: the core and the structural column grid “rotate” together with the building façade.

All offices at AIR maintain correct geometry and commercial efficiency. At the same time, each floor — with its staircases and utilities — is slightly rotated around the vertical axis relative to the previous one.

Headquarters for visionaries

The distinctive architecture of AIR effectively communicates the image and values of any progressive corporation. Recognizing this PR and GR potential, we designed the third building as the most advanced headquarters in the Leningradsky business district.

B3

1 / 3

The 14-story tower is designed specifically as the headquarters of a large company and is fully autonomous.

Its engineering systems are either independent (ventilation and air conditioning), supplied via separate networks (water and heating), or designed to be isolated when necessary (servers and dispatch systems).

A company that establishes its headquarters in AIR’s third tower can shape its own service infrastructure and independently define the building management model and contractors.

Service excellence

Privileges define status.
At AIR, exceptional attention to residents and care for their well-being and privacy are felt throughout every space.

Wide parking driveways, silent VIP elevators, adaptive climate control, and high-speed Wi-Fi across the entire business center — pursuing ambitious goals here is not only prestigious, but genuinely comfortable.

From electric vehicle charging stations to advanced security technologies, premium service at AIR is elevated to a guiding principle.

Premium
technologies

High-speed elevators

Sword elevators with speeds of up to 5 m/s and a maximum waiting time of 40 seconds. Zoning of elevator groups in Buildings 1 and 2 reduces waiting times, minimizes stops, and improves ride comfort.

Spacious underground parking

An underground parking facility with 490 spaces. Ceiling height in the main driveways on level –1 is 3.1 m, ensuring comfortable vehicle access and convenient unloading for commercial spaces.

VIP elevator for top management

Priority access for executives and top management of companies occupying large office blocks. Travel to the required floor is direct, without transit stops.

Drop-off zone

The project provides a convenient area for passenger pick-up and drop-off at the building entrance.

Increased number of turnstiles

Multiple elevator zones in each tower reduce waiting times, minimize stops, and enhance ride comfort.

Electric vehicle charging stations

Dedicated spaces with charging stations or the option to install them are provided.

Advanced security technologies

  • 24/7 security service and video surveillance;
  • access control using Face ID, smartphones, and QR codes;
  • BLE, NFC, and QR turnstiles with resident priority access;
  • vehicle license plate recognition system and RFID tags

Innovative
engineering

Dedicated service elevators

Separate elevators for building operations and office servicing.

Central air conditioning and ventilation systems

Year-round centralized air conditioning with a winter cooling function, along with a central supply-and-exhaust ventilation system featuring two-stage air filtration. Air exchange of at least 60 m³/hour per person.

Seamless Wi-Fi and enhanced mobile coverage

Internet access throughout the entire business center, including elevators, parking, lobbies, and landscaped areas, supported by a mobile signal amplification system.

Intelligent building management system

Building Management System (BMS) — a hardware and software solution for monitoring and coordinating all building processes.

Efficient power supply

Dedicated electrical capacity of at least 100 W per sq m for office spaces. Three-phase power connection.

Automated fire safety systems

Smoke detectors, evacuation alert and control systems, automatic fire suppression, and smoke extraction systems in all areas.

of successful
business

AIR meets the CLEVER eco-certification standards at the Gold level. The practices applied in the design, construction, and operation of the towers comply with ESG principles.

The environmental performance of the premium business center aligns with the internal policies of progressive corporations, as well as the requirements of their regulators and industries. Most importantly, it creates a comfortable and healthy working environment for every employee.
