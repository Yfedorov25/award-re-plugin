Modern
business cluster

The modern business district is among the top three areas for commercial rental growth. Every third office search in the city is concentrated here. The area faces a significant shortage of both small office spaces and standalone buildings suitable for corporate headquarters.

Located between the Mall and Nature Park, AIR offers next-generation premium offices — aesthetic and functional, with advanced engineering and a high-quality urban environment.

Transport accessibility

AIR residents benefit from direct access to one of the city’s key arteries allowing quick travel to both the city center and Airport. Proximity to the Highway also connects AIR to other business districts
